About Shem

Landscape and nature photographer, Pacific Northwest

I'm Shem — a landscape and nature photographer based in the Pacific Northwest. Most of what I shoot starts before sunrise: coastline, old-growth forest, and the kind of light that only shows up for a few minutes at a time.

I got into photography the way a lot of people do — by taking a camera on a trip I didn't want to forget, and never really putting it down again. Since then it's grown from a personal habit into portrait sessions, prints, and the occasional editorial assignment.

These days I split my time between personal landscape work, location portrait sessions, and writing about both in the journal. If you'd like to work together, the contact page is the fastest way to reach me.
