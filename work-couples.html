How a proposal works

  1. Plan it together

    Message me on WhatsApp with your date and idea. We pick the spot, the time and a signal — and I can help arrange flowers, a sign or dinner.

  2. You ask

    For a secret proposal I’m hidden in position before you arrive and capture the question and the reaction unseen. Prefer it open? I’m there with you from the start.

    Straight after the yes, a relaxed shoot for the two of you. Your edited photos follow within a week.
